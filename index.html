Ashish Uploader

A personal tool for posting my own videos to TikTok

Ashish Uploader is a small, self-hosted tool that I use to publish videos I have created to my own TikTok account. It runs on my own computer and uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. I sign in with TikTok once and authorize the app to post on my behalf.
  2. I put finished videos, each with a caption, into a local queue folder.
  3. For each video, the tool reads my current TikTok creator settings (privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length), then shows me the post details to confirm.
  4. The video is uploaded through the Content Posting API, either directly to my profile or to my TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the app.

Who can use it

This tool is for my personal use only. It is not offered to the public, and no other TikTok accounts are connected to it.
